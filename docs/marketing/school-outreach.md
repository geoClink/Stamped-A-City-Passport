# School outreach drafts

Drafts for you to send yourself. Send them in this order, and only after Michigan State's page is
filled out, since that page is what every email points to.

1. Student groups at Michigan State (tour guides, traditions, architecture students, the student paper)
2. High school counselors and parent groups
3. Michigan State itself, with what the student groups said

Fill in the `[brackets]`. Find each group's current contact on its own site or MSU's student
organization directory; don't guess addresses.

Before sending, open the school page on your phone and check it top to bottom. Every email links
to it, so a wrong fact there costs you the reply.
