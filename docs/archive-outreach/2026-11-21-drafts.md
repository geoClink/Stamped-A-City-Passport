# Live cameras

## Rules

- **In the app:** showing a YouTube live stream through YouTube's own embedded player is allowed by YouTube's terms. The player has to keep its controls and YouTube link visible, meet a minimum size, not autoplay off-screen, and identify the app (set the Referer, or the `origin` parameter). If the owner has turned off embedding, it won't play.
- **In your own YouTube videos or Shorts:** reusing someone's camera footage needs their permission. EarthCam says outright that its feeds can't be rebroadcast without permission. A licensed YouTube Live use needs the credit "Courtesy of EarthCam", a link, and its logo left visible.
- **Linking** to a camera page is always fine.
