## Flagged during research

Items the research couldn't fully confirm, or that need a judgment call. Everything else below was

### Removed on purpose
Memorials to people who died don't belong in a checklist game, so these were taken out of the
traditions: Virginia Tech's April 16 Memorial and the Pylons, Texas A&M's Bonfire Memorial, Clemson's
Scroll of Honor, Arizona's Berger Memorial Fountain and USS Arizona bell, Oxford's Martyrs' Cross,
St Andrews' Hamilton's Face. Also left out: anything with alcohol, swimming, climbing, nudity,
students-only events, hazing, mocking another school, and Syracuse's Saltine Warrior statue.

### University of Melbourne

### University of Oxford

- [ ] Tradition: **Great Tom's 101 Strokes**: Every night at 9:05pm (9pm old 'Oxford time') the Great Tom bell in Christ Church's Tom Tower rings 101 times, once for each of the college's original 100 scholars plus one added in 1663. - [ ] Tradition: **May Morning at Magdalen Tower**: At 6am on 1 May the Magdalen College Choir sings the Hymnus Eucharisticus from the top of the Great Tower, a ceremony going back about 500 years. - [ ] Tradition: **Bridge of Sighs**: Hertford Bridge, nicknamed the Bridge of Sighs after the Venice bridge it is said to resemble, links two parts of Hertford College over New College Lane. ### University of Padua

### University of Sydney
