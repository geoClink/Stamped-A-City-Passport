# Executive Summary
## Stamped! for Michigan State University

### Overview
`Stamped! for Michigan State University` is a campus exploration app concept designed to help new students learn where important places are, what they are for, and how to navigate campus with more confidence. Adapted from `Stamped! A City Passport`, the concept transforms orientation from a passive information experience into an interactive system of discovery, progress, and reward.

### The Opportunity
New students often arrive on campus with limited familiarity with key locations such as dining halls, parking areas, academic buildings, residence halls, libraries, and student support services. While orientation materials provide useful information, they do not always create lasting spatial familiarity or repeated engagement with the physical campus. As a result, students can feel overwhelmed, uncertain, and disconnected during the first weeks of the semester.

### The Proposed Solution
`Stamped! for Michigan State University` would provide a mobile-first, gamified campus experience that helps students explore and learn the university environment in a more engaging way. Students would browse important campus destinations, complete exploration challenges, answer short orientation quizzes, and unlock progress badges or stamps as they build familiarity with campus.

The platform could be organized around practical student needs, including:

- academic buildings
- dining halls and cafeterias
- parking lots and transportation access
- residence halls
- libraries and study spaces
- student services
- recreation and wellness spaces
- campus landmarks and traditions

### Why It Matters
This concept is designed to support both student success and institutional communication goals.

For students, it could:

- reduce early-semester navigation anxiety
- improve awareness of important campus resources
- create a more engaging orientation experience
- encourage exploration beyond the classroom
- strengthen confidence and sense of belonging

For Michigan State University, it could:

- modernize part of the orientation experience
- increase visibility of high-value campus services
- provide a scalable digital engagement tool for first-year students
- support student transition and campus familiarity efforts
- offer a pilot-friendly concept that can begin with limited scope

### Why Stamped! Is a Strong Fit
The existing `Stamped!` framework already includes the core interaction model needed for this use case: discovery, progress tracking, quizzes, completion rewards, and mobile-first browsing. This makes the MSU concept more than an abstract idea. It is an adaptation of an existing product structure that can be tailored to the campus environment.

### Recommended Pilot
A pilot version could focus on 20 to 40 high-value campus destinations and a small set of orientation tracks for first-year students. Initial categories could include dining, parking, academic support, student services, and key campus landmarks. This would allow the university to assess student engagement and usefulness before considering broader rollout.

### Next Step
The next step would be a conversation about whether Michigan State University would be interested in exploring a pilot or concept review for a campus-specific version of `Stamped!`.

### Contact Purpose
This executive summary is intended to introduce the concept, demonstrate its relevance to student onboarding, and open discussion around a possible partnership, pilot, or exploratory review.
