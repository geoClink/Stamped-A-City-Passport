Concept Proposal

Stamped! for Michigan State University

A campus exploration app concept that turns freshman orientation, wayfinding, and resource literacy into a gamified discovery experience.

Primary Use Case
Help new students learn campus faster and with less friction
Built From
The existing Stamped! discovery, quiz, progress, and reward framework
Proposal Type
Pilot-ready student experience concept
Opportunity

The Problem

Orientation materials explain campus, but they do not always create lasting familiarity with the places students actually need to use.

1

New students arrive with low spatial familiarity.

2

Important services can stay invisible until a student urgently needs them.

3

Static maps are useful but passive.

4

Confidence grows through repeated discovery, not just one-time explanation.

Core Question

What if campus orientation worked less like a packet and more like an interactive exploration system that students actually wanted to complete?

Solution

The Product

Stamped! can be adapted from city discovery to campus discovery without changing its strongest interaction model.

Use Cases

What Students Would Do

The app supports both pre-arrival exploration and on-campus orientation during the first weeks of the semester.

1

Browse

Explore key places by category, district, or student need.

2

Learn

Read short explanations of what each place is for and why students use it.

3

Visit

Complete discovery challenges tied to real campus destinations.

4

Quiz

Reinforce memory with short orientation and wayfinding questions.

5

Collect

Unlock badges or stamps for orientation tracks and campus milestones.

Value

Why This Matters To MSU

The concept supports both student experience goals and practical communication goals.

Student Benefits

  • Lower navigation anxiety during the first semester
  • Faster familiarity with essential campus resources
  • More engaging orientation experience
  • Better awareness of support services and study spaces
  • Stronger sense of progress and belonging

Institutional Benefits

  • A more interactive orientation layer without requiring every touchpoint to be staff-led
  • Better visibility for services students may otherwise overlook
  • A reusable digital framework for onboarding, tours, and student engagement
  • A concept that can start small and scale later

Best Fit

This concept is strongest as a pilot for first-year orientation, transfer onboarding, or a guided “learn your campus” initiative.

Pilot

Recommended First Version

A pilot should prove usefulness quickly instead of trying to map every part of campus at once.

Scope

  • 20 to 40 destinations
  • 5 to 8 campus categories
  • Orientation quiz mode
  • Badge or stamp tracking

Content

  • Names and short descriptions
  • Coordinates or map references
  • Hours where relevant
  • Accessibility notes

Use Cases

  • Freshman orientation
  • Transfer student onboarding
  • Campus essentials challenge
  • Resource awareness campaign
Phase Goal Output
Phase 1 Define campus content set Approved destinations, descriptions, categories
Phase 2 Create campus-branded prototype MSU-specific mock screens and navigation flows

Pitch Positioning

Lead with the student problem, then show that the product framework already exists and can be adapted.

Suggested Message

Stamped! for Michigan State University is a campus exploration concept designed to help new students learn where things are, what they are for, and how to navigate campus with more confidence.

Instead of a passive orientation tool, it creates a guided system of discovery, progress, and reward.

Important Note

This deck presents a concept proposal for MSU use. It should not be presented as an official partnership or approved branded product unless the university agrees to move forward.

What Strengthens This Proposal

The core app structure already exists. This is not a pitch for an abstract idea; it is a pitch for adapting a working discovery framework to a high-value campus use case.
