## Flagged during research

Items the research couldn't fully confirm, or that need a judgment call. Everything else below was
checked against the source linked next to it.

### Removed on purpose
Memorials to people who died don't belong in a checklist game, so these were taken out of the
traditions: Virginia Tech's April 16 Memorial and the Pylons, Texas A&M's Bonfire Memorial, Clemson's
Scroll of Honor, Arizona's Berger Memorial Fountain and USS Arizona bell, Oxford's Martyrs' Cross,
St Andrews' Hamilton's Face. Also left out: anything with alcohol, swimming, climbing, nudity,
students-only events, hazing, mocking another school, and Syracuse's Saltine Warrior statue.
