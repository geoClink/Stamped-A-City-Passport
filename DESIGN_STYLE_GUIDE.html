Stamped! A City Passport

Design Style Guide

A visual system for an editorial, travel-inspired architecture app built around discovery, progress, stamps, and collected city memories.

Foundation

Brand Direction

The app should feel collected, warm, and worldly rather than generic or overly technical.

Brand Summary

Stamped! should feel like a modern city passport with hints of museum signage, travel ephemera, and ceremonial achievement.

Curious Collected Worldly Tactile Reward-driven Archival

Avoid

Do not drift into generic tech-dashboard styling.
Do not flood screens with accent color.
Do not use playful cartoon illustration as the primary visual mode.
Do not overanimate navigation and core reading screens.

Design Principles

01

Use one strong accent color consistently instead of several competing brand colors.

02

Let typography create hierarchy before decorative effects do.

03

Favor grouped surfaces and generous spacing to keep dense information readable.

04

Reward states should feel ceremonial: stamps, seals, progress, and completion marks.

05

Accessibility settings simplify contrast and motion without replacing the visual system.

06

Every screen should reinforce travel, place, and collected architectural memory.

Rule Guidance
Accent discipline Orange is the thread tying together City List, City Detail, Quiz, Passport, and Building Detail.
Semantic states Green means success only. Red means destructive or incorrect only. Gold is for mastery and trophy moments.
High contrast mode Replace decorative color coding with strong primary text, visible outlines, and system semantic colors.
Backgrounds Use semantic grouped/system backgrounds for most shells instead of flat brand fills.

Type Rules

Use serif only for destination identity and ceremonial moments. Keep body copy in sentence case. Use monospaced labels for progress, stats, and field-data moments. Dynamic Type should stack layout before aggressive text compression occurs.

Shape Language

Major hero and grouped surfaces: 20-24 pt radius
Passport and stamp cards: 15-20 pt radius
Utility controls: 8-12 pt radius or capsule
High contrast mode: reduce decorative softness, strengthen outlines

Imagery Direction

  • Use real architecture and city photography rather than illustration.
  • Favor crops that emphasize structure, material, silhouette, and scale.
  • Avoid generic lifestyle stock images.
  • Default image treatment should be immersive and edge-to-edge.
  • When images are missing, fallback states should remain branded and instructional.

Current Asset Reality

The catalog is intentionally light. It currently includes the app icon set and the named color asset adventureOrange. Building images are referenced in code through building.assetName, so photography consistency matters more than asset-catalog ornament.

System

Components and Feedback

The UI should feel like a set of collected artifacts rather than flat interchangeable cards.

Buttons

  • Primary CTA: filled brand orange, white label, bold weight, large control size.
  • Secondary action: tinted orange background at low opacity with orange text.
  • Destructive action: system red only in resets and irreversible flows.

Cards

  • Use light semantic surfaces with 20-24 pt padding.
  • Keep shadows soft in standard mode and replace them with borders in high contrast.
  • Cards should feel dossier-like, grouped, and tactile.

Progress

  • Tint with brand orange.
  • Pair color with explicit text value.
  • Increase thickness when the moment is meaningful.

Stamp Motif

  • Dashed borders, commemorative copy, paper-like field.
  • Slight rotation is allowed when motion settings permit it.
  • Stamp visuals are the most distinct part of the product identity.

Quiz Dossier

  • Structured metadata labels and serif prompt headline.
  • Answer buttons must feel decisive, large, and high-contrast.
  • Celebration overlays can be bolder than standard content surfaces.

Motion and Feedback

  • Use spring for reveal, expansion, and tap response.
  • Reserve scale and ceremonial transitions for success states.
  • Respect Reduce Motion by swapping to opacity or no animation.
Haptics should reinforce selection and milestones. Sound should be ceremonial and optional, especially around stamp completion.
Screens

App-Wide Application

Each major screen family should express the same visual system through its own role.

Onboarding

  • Cinematic dark gradient with orange glow.
  • Large welcoming type and symbolic iconography.
  • Primary CTA remains obvious and warm.

City List

  • Should read like an organized atlas sidebar.
  • Orange appears in active states, icons, and progress.
  • Welcome placeholder remains spacious and icon-led.

City Detail

  • Editorial city dossier tone.
  • Serif city title and ceremonial nickname label.
  • Grouped cards and clear dividers between sections.

Building Detail

  • Hero photography leads the screen.
  • User photo capture tools should feel integrated, not bolted on.
  • Metadata should read like museum placard content.

Quiz

  • Field-investigation tone with dossier structure.
  • Visible score and progress at all times.
  • Correct and incorrect states stay semantically clean.

Passport

  • Most branded screen family in the app.
  • Lean into archive, collection, and reward metaphors.
  • Completed states should feel collectible and ceremonial.

Accessibility Rules

Minimum tap target is 44 by 44 points. Meaning should never rely on color alone. Dynamic Type must preserve hierarchy by stacking and expanding rather than clipping.

High contrast mode should emphasize outlines, primary text, and simplified fills. Reduce Motion should remove tilt and large spring behaviors while preserving structure through opacity and timing.
